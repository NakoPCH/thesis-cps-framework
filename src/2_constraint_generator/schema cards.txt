### DSL Rules: Area Goals (area_goals.tx)
* Allowed AreaGoalTag: `ENTER` | `EXIT` | `AVOID` | `STEP` (All area goals terminate with `end`).

* Goal<RectangleArea>:
  - Required fields: `bottomLeftEdge: Point`, `lengthX: <number>`, `lengthY: <number>`, `tag: <AreaGoalTag>`
  - Optional fields: `entities:` (hyphenated list: `- <Entity>`), `timeConstraints:` (hyphenated list)
  - FORBIDDEN fields: `entity:` (singular forbidden; use plural `entities:`)

* Goal<CircularArea>:
  - Required fields: `center: Point`, `radius: <number>`, `tag: <AreaGoalTag>`
  - Optional fields: `entities:` (hyphenated list: `- <Entity>`), `timeConstraints:` (hyphenated list)
  - FORBIDDEN fields: `position:`, `entity:` (use `center:`, and plural `entities:`)

* Goal<PolylineArea>:
  - Required fields: `points: [Point, Point, ...]`, `tag: <AreaGoalTag>`
  - Optional fields: `entities:` (hyphenated list: `- <Entity>`), `timeConstraints:` (hyphenated list)
  - FORBIDDEN fields: `entity:` (use plural `entities:`)

* Goal<MovingArea>:
  - Required fields: `movingEntity: <Entity>`, `radius: <number>`, `tag: <AreaGoalTag>`
  - Optional fields: `entities:` (hyphenated list: `- <Entity>`), `timeConstraints:` (hyphenated list)
  - Note: `movingEntity:` takes a single ID; `entities:` is the target list

* Goal<StraightLine>:
  - Required fields: `startPoint: Point`, `endPoint: Point`, `tag: <AreaGoalTag>`
  - Optional fields: `entities:` (hyphenated list: `- <Entity>`), `timeConstraints:` (hyphenated list)
  - FORBIDDEN fields: `from:`, `to:`, `start:`, `end:`, `entity:` (use `startPoint:` and `endPoint:`)


### DSL Rules: Communication & Brokers (communication.tx)
* All Broker and RESTEndpoint blocks terminate with `end`.
* Authentication formats (`auth:` sub-block):
  - Plain: `username: '<str>'` and `password: '<str>'`
  - ApiKey: `key: '<str>'`
  - Cert: `cert: '<str>'` OR `certPath: '<str>'`

* Broker<AMQP>:
  - Required fields: `host: '<str>'`, `port: <int>`
  - Optional fields: `vhost: '<str>'`, `topicExchange: '<str>'`, `rpcExchange: '<str>'`, `ssl: <true|false>`, `auth:`

* Broker<MQTT>:
  - Required fields: `host: '<str>'`, `port: <int>`
  - Optional fields: `ssl: <true|false>`, `basePath: '<str>'`, `webPath: '<str>'`, `webPort: <int>`, `auth:`

* Broker<Redis>:
  - Required fields: `host: '<str>'`, `port: <int>`
  - Optional fields: `db: <int>`, `ssl: <true|false>`, `auth:`

* RESTEndpoint:
  - Allowed RESTVerb (`verb:`): `GET` | `POST` | `PUT` | `DELETE`
  - Required fields: `verb: <RESTVerb>`, `host: '<str>'`, `port: <int>`, `path: '<str>'`
  - Optional fields: `base_url: '<str>'`, `params:` (sub-keys: `query:`, `path:`, `body:`, `headers:`)


### DSL Rules: Conditions & Expressions (condition.tx)
* Standalone Construct:
  - TermPool: `TermPool <Name>` containing one or more conditions, ending with `end`.

* Condition Structure & Grouping:
  - Parentheses: Sub-conditions and comparisons must be enclosed in parentheses: `(<expr> <op> <expr>)`.
  - Logical Operators: `and` | `or` | `not` | `xor` | `nor` | `xnor` | `nand` (FORBIDDEN: `&&`, `||`, `!`).

* Attribute Referencing:
  - Simple: `<Entity>.<attribute>`
  - Dict: `<Entity>.<attribute>['<key>']`
  - List: `<Entity>.<attribute>[<index>]`

* Comparison Operators:
  - Numeric & Time: `>=` | `>` | `<=` | `<` | `==` | `!=` | `is` | `is not`
  - String: `==` | `!=` | `is` | `is not` | `~` | `!~` | `has` | `in` | `not in`
  - Boolean: `==` | `!=` | `is` | `is not` (values: `true` | `false`)
  - List & Dict: `==` | `!=` | `is` | `is not` (Lists also support `in` | `not in`)

* Built-in Numeric Functions:
  - `inrange(<operand>, <min>, <max>)`
  - `std(<operand>, <size>)` | `var(<operand>, <size>)` | `mean(<operand>, <size>)` | `min(<operand>, <size>)` | `max(<operand>, <size>)`


### DSL Rules: Entities (entity.tx)
* All Entity blocks begin with `Entity Name` and terminate with `end`.
* Allowed EntityType (`type:`): `sensor` | `actuator` | `hybrid` | `robot` | `other`.
* Allowed EntityMode (`mode:`): `virtual` | `simulation` | `real` | `digitwin` | `other`.

* Entity:
  - Required fields: `type: <EntityType>`, `source: <BrokerOrEndpointName>`
  - Optional fields: `uri: '<str>'`, `mode: <EntityMode>`, `freq: <number>`, `description: '<str>'`, `attributes:`, `compartments ... end`
  - Target source: `source:` must reference a declared MessageBroker (AMQP, MQTT, Redis) or RESTEndpoint

* Attributes (`attributes:` sub-block):
  - Format: Hyphenated list `- <name>: <DataType>`
  - Optional precision and multiplicity: `<name>: <DataType>(<precision>)[<bounds>]`


### DSL Rules: Entity Goals (entity_goals.tx)
* All entity goals terminate with `end`.

* Goal<EntityStateChange>:
  - Required fields: `entity: <Entity>`
  - Optional fields: `timeConstraints:` (hyphenated list)
  - Note: Uses singular `entity:`, not plural `entities:`

* Goal<EntityStateCondition>:
  - Required fields: `condition: <Condition>`
  - Optional fields: `timeConstraints:` (hyphenated list)
  - FORBIDDEN fields: `entity:` (Target entities are defined inside condition expressions)

* Goal<EntityPyCondition>:
  - Required fields: `condition: '<str>'`
  - Optional fields: `timeConstraints:` (hyphenated list)
  - FORBIDDEN fields: `entity:`

* Goal<EntityAttrStream>:
  - Allowed strategies (`strategy:`): `ALL` | `NONE` | `AT_LEAST_ONE` | `JUST_ONE` | `EXACTLY_X` | `ALL_ORDERED` | `EXACTLY_X_ORDERED`
  - Required fields: `stream: <Entity>.<attr>: [<val1>, <val2>, ...]`, `strategy: <Strategy>`
  - Optional fields: `timeConstraints:` (hyphenated list)

* Goal<EntityStateTransition>:
  - Required fields: `entity: <Entity>`, `previous: <Condition>`, `next: <Condition>`
  - Optional fields: `timeConstraints:` (hyphenated list)


### DSL Rules: Top-Level Model, Metadata & Scenario (goal_dsl.tx)
* All standalone blocks terminate with `end`.

* Metadata:
  - Required fields: `name: <ID>`, `version: '<str>'`
  - Optional fields: `author: '<str>'`, `email: '<str>'`, `description: '<str>'`

* RTMonitor:
  - Required fields: `broker: <BrokerName>`
  - Optional fields: `namespace: '<str>'`, `eventTopic: '<str>'`, `logsTopic: '<str>'`

* Scenario:
  - Required fields: `goals:` (hyphenated list: `- <Goal>` or `- <Goal> -> <weight>`)
  - Optional fields: `antigoals:` (hyphenated list: `- <Goal>` or `- <Goal> -> <weight>`), `fatals:` (hyphenated list: `- <Goal>`), `timeConstraints:` (hyphenated list), `concurrent: <true|false>`, `tickFreq: <number>`, `description: '<str>'`, `namespace: '<str>'`
  - FORBIDDEN fields: Weights in `fatals:` (use only `- <Goal>`, never `-> <weight>`)


### DSL Rules: Complex & Composition Goals (goal.tx)
* All composition goal blocks terminate with `end`[cite: 11].

* Goal<Complex>:
  - Allowed strategies (`strategy:`): `ALL_ACCOMPLISHED` | `NONE_ACCOMPLISHED` | `AT_LEAST_ONE_ACCOMPLISHED` | `JUST_ONE_ACCOMPLISHED` | `EXACTLY_X_ACCOMPLISHED` | `ALL_ACCOMPLISHED_ORDERED` | `EXACTLY_X_ACCOMPLISHED_ORDERED`[cite: 11]
  - Required fields: `goals:` (hyphenated list: `- <Goal>` or `- <Goal> -> <weight>`), `strategy: <strategy>`[cite: 11]
  - Optional fields: `xAccomplished: <int>`, `timeConstraints:` (hyphenated list)[cite: 11]
  - Note: Use `xAccomplished:` strictly when using `EXACTLY_X_*` strategies[cite: 11].

* Goal<Repeater>:
  - Required fields: `goal: <Goal>`, `times: <int>`[cite: 11]
  - Optional fields: `timeConstraints:` (hyphenated list)[cite: 11]
  - FORBIDDEN fields: `goals:` (use singular `goal:` with a single target)[cite: 11]

* Goal<GTerm>:
  - Required fields: `terms:` (hyphenated list of logic terms: `- (<Goal1> and <Goal2>)` or `- <Goal>`)[cite: 11]
  - Optional fields: `operator: <LogicalOperator>`, `timeConstraints:` (hyphenated list)[cite: 11]


### DSL Rules: Pose, Orientation & Position Goals (pose_goals.tx)
* All goal blocks terminate with `end`.

* Goal<Position>:
  - Required fields: `entity: <Entity>`, `position: <Point>`, `maxDeviation: <number>`
  - Optional fields: `timeConstraints:` (hyphenated list)
  - FORBIDDEN fields: `target:` (use `position:`), `maxDeviationPos:` (use `maxDeviation:`)

* Goal<Orientation>:
  - Required fields: `entity: <Entity>`, `orientation: <Orientation>`, `maxDeviation: <number>`
  - Optional fields: `timeConstraints:` (hyphenated list)
  - FORBIDDEN fields: `maxDeviationOri:` (use `maxDeviation:`)

* Goal<Pose>:
  - Required fields: `entity: <Entity>`, `position: <Point>`, `orientation: <Orientation>`, `maxDeviationPos: <number>`, `maxDeviationOri: <number>`
  - Optional fields: `timeConstraints:` (hyphenated list)
  - FORBIDDEN fields: `maxDeviation:` (must explicitly specify separate `maxDeviationPos:` and `maxDeviationOri:`)


### DSL Rules: Time Constraints (time_constraint.tx)
* Applied inside `timeConstraints:` blocks as a hyphenated list (`- <Constraint>`).

* TimeConstraint Structure:
  - Syntax: `<Type>(<optional_comparator><float_time>)`
  - Allowed Types (`type`): `FROM_SCENARIO_START` | `FROM_GOAL_START` | `FOR_TIME` | `BETWEEN_GOALS_MIN` | `BETWEEN_GOALS_MAX`
  - Allowed Comparators (`comparator`): `>` | `<` | `==` | `>=` | `<=`
  - Examples:
    - `- FROM_GOAL_START(<60)`
    - `- FOR_TIME(5)`
    - `- FROM_SCENARIO_START(<=180.0)`


### DSL Rules: Trajectory Goals (trajectory_goals.tx)
* All trajectory goal blocks terminate with `end`.

* Goal<StraightLineTrajectory>:
  - Required fields: `entity: <Entity>`, `startPoint: <Point>`, `finishPoint: <Point>`, `maxDeviation: <number>`
  - Optional fields: `timeConstraints:` (hyphenated list)
  - FORBIDDEN fields: `endPoint:` (use `finishPoint:`), `entities:` (use singular `entity:`)

* Goal<WaypointTrajectory>:
  - Required fields: `entity: <Entity>`, `points: [<Point>, <Point>, ...]`, `maxDeviation: <number>`
  - Optional fields: `timeConstraints:` (hyphenated list)
  - Note: `points:` requires comma-separated points inside square brackets `[...]`

* Goal<CurveTrajectoryGoal>:
  - Required fields: `entity: <Entity>`, `startPoint: <Point>`, `finishPoint: <Point>`, `curvature: <number>`, `maxDeviation: <number>`
  - Optional fields: `timeConstraints:` (hyphenated list)
  - FORBIDDEN fields: `endPoint:` (use `finishPoint:`)


### DSL Rules: Types & Primitives (types.tx)
* Spatial & Geometric Literals:
  - Point2D: `Point2D(<x>, <y>)`
  - Point3D: `Point3D(<x>, <y>, <z>)`
  - Orientation2D: `Orientation2D(<z>)`
  - Orientation3D: `Orientation3D(<x>, <y>, <z>)`

* Collection Literals:
  - List: `[<item1>, <item2>, ...]` (items: number, string, bool, list, dict)
  - Dict: `{'<key>': <value>, ...}` (keys MUST be strings enclosed in quotes)

* Temporal Formats:
  - Time: `<HH>:<MM>` or `<HH>:<MM>:<SS>`
  - Date: `<MM>:<DD>:<YYYY>`

* Declarations (terminate with `end`):
  - DataType: `DataType <Name> ... properties ... end`
    - Simple property: `<name>: <DataType>` (optional default: `= '<str>'`)
    - List property: `<name>: <DataType>[<multiplicity>]` (multiplicity: `<int>` or `*`)
  - Enum: `Enum <Name> '<Element1>' '<Element2>' ... end` (elements are string literals)


### DSL Rules: Utilities & Lexical Rules (utils.tx)
* Module Imports:
  - Syntax: `import <FQN>` or `import <FQN> as <ID>`

* Comments:
  - Single-line: `// <comment text>`
  - Multi-line: `/* <comment text> */`

* Multi-line Strings:
  - Triple quotes: `"""<string content>"""`

* Identifiers & Naming:
  - Dot-notation paths (FQN): `name` or `pkg.name` or `pkg.sub.name`
  - Reserved Keywords: `import` cannot be used as an identifier (`<ID>`)
